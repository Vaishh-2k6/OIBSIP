Biography

A Life of Grace and Duty

Birth in Ayodhya

Prince Rama was born in the sacred city of Ayodhya, where joy and hope filled the palace and the people celebrated the arrival of a child destined to become a beacon of righteousness.

Prince of Kosala

As the beloved son of King Dasharatha, he grew up as the prince of Kosala, known for his calm wisdom, disciplined mind, and gentle strength that made every heart feel safe in his presence.

An Ideal Son and Brother

Rama’s devotion to his father and his affectionate bond with Lakshmana and Sita revealed a rare depth of character; his life showed that duty can be fulfilled without losing tenderness or humility.

Exile and Victory of Dharma

When circumstances demanded sacrifice, he accepted exile with dignity, stood beside Sita and Lakshmana, and ultimately defeated Ravana to restore peace, proving that truth and justice prevail when upheld with courage and compassion.

His glorious return marked the restoration of peace and the beginning of a golden age.

Legacy

How His Light Continues to Inspire

The Ramayana remains one of the most cherished epics in the world, shaping festivals such as Diwali, inspiring temples, art, dance, literature, and countless reflections on leadership and moral duty.

Across India and beyond, Lord Rama’s example continues to influence culture, spiritual thought, and the way people understand humility, courage, and the pursuit of righteousness.

May the ideals of Lord Rama continue to inspire righteousness, humility and courage.
